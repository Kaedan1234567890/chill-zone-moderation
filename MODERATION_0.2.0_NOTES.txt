Chill Zone Moderation 0.2.0-alpha-punishments

Added:
- /tempban <player> <offence> preset workflow with known/offline player autocomplete.
- Preset offence autocomplete includes duration tooltips.
- Presets: flying 3d, xray 7d, combat 7d, godmode 7d, duping 14d,
  major_griefing 7d, minor_griefing 1d, event_disruption 3d, harassment 3d,
  chat_spam 12h, ban_evasion 14d, severe_exploiting 14d.
- Stealing intentionally NOT included.
- Hacking requires one or more hack types: e.g.
  /tempban Player hacking flying,phase_noclip,godmode
  Hacking preset duration: 7d.
- Custom punishment:
  /tempban Player other 5d Custom reason here
- /punishments and /punishments <player> show active ban type, reason,
  remaining time, and issuing moderator.
- /unban <player> and existing /czunban <player> clear both temp/permanent bans.
- /czban and /ban support reason autocomplete while still allowing custom text.
- Known-player suggestions use online players + vanilla usercache.json, so offline
  players who previously joined can be selected.

Existing LuckPerms nodes preserved:
  chillzonemoderation.tempban
  chillzonemoderation.ban
  chillzonemoderation.unban
  chillzonemoderation.warnings  (also gates /punishments)

NOTE: /sus anti-cheat/reporting changes are intentionally NOT part of this build;
that was planned as the next moderation update after the punishment workflow.
