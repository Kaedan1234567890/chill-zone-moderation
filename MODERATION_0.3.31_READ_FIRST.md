# Moderation 0.3.31 — Rule 33 wording only

Starting from 0.3.30, replaces the text of the No Naked Killing rule with protection for unarmored players, allowing initiation after the player equips at least three armor pieces, and retaining self-defense against an unarmored aggressor. The rest of the rules and commands are unchanged. This is a text-rule update, not automatic combat prevention. Source only; build and gameplay testing are not completed.
