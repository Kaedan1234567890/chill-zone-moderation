Chill Zone Moderation 0.2.6 - SUS Activity Split

- Keeps /czban, /czunban, /tempban and /untempban unchanged.
- Keeps blacklist/login enforcement from 0.2.4/0.2.5.
- Keeps existing SUS diamond and Ancient Debris detection.
- Adds Hacks Activity as its own Live Stats/SUS case.
- Fly/anti-fly data no longer appears on Diamond or Ancient Debris cases.
- Hacks Activity shows only fly-hack evidence.
- Players can appear in more than one activity category if they trigger more than one detector.
- Clear This SUS Case clears only the selected activity category, including Hacks Activity.
- /sus <player> prioritizes Hacks Activity when the player has recorded flight attempts.
- Mining lore was shortened to fit the Minecraft inventory tooltip more cleanly.
