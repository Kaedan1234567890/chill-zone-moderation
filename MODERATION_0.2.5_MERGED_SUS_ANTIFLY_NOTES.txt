Chill Zone Moderation 0.2.5 alpha - Merged SUS + Anti-Fly
- Keeps /czban and /czunban names unchanged.
- Keeps timed/permanent UUID login blacklist from 0.2.4 FIX1.
- Merges the existing Chill Zone SUS mining/X-ray investigation system into Moderation.
- /sus and /susclear are now supplied by the Moderation mod; remove the old standalone SUS jar to avoid duplicate commands/listeners.
- Adds always-on conservative anti-fly for Survival/Adventure.
- Creative, Spectator, Elytra, passenger, water and climbable movement are exempt.
- Anti-fly does not auto-ban. Confirmed attempted unsupported flight is blocked and recorded in /sus.
- /sus shows Fly Hacks YES/NO, attempts, prevented attempts, successful illegal flight, and last fly attempt.
