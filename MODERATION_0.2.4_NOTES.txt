CHILL ZONE MODERATION 0.2.4 - LOGIN BLACKLIST

- Version changed to 0.2.4-alpha-login-blacklist.
- Existing command names are preserved, including /czban and /czunban.
- /czban creates a permanent punishment that is enforced at the PlayerList login gate by UUID.
- /tempban creates a timed punishment enforced by the same UUID login gate.
- Expired temp bans are automatically cleared when the player next attempts to connect.
- /czunban and /untempban clear the stored punishment, which removes the blacklist block.
- The existing post-join disconnect check remains as a second failsafe.
- Disconnect screen preserves reason, live remaining time for temp bans, Discord appeal information, and now explicitly shows Punished By.
- Existing warnings, mutes, punishment history, offence presets, hack subtype combinations, and autocomplete are retained.
